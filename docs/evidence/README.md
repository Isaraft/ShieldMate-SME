# Engineering evidence, not human research

Files here are deliberately retained developer/unit/browser/Docker verification artifacts. App screenshots and PDFs under phase directories use explicit synthetic QA or DEMO fixtures; they are not proof of customer assessments, participants, consent or a completed beta study. See per-phase reports and captions for exact scope and warnings.

Phase 10 redacts personal home/checkout prefixes from current text logs and documentation. Test counts, outcomes and dates are retained; path placeholders are not executable local setup instructions. Original blobs remain in Git history: this cleanup does not make history anonymously publishable. The redacted audit reports identify affected paths/categories without disclosing matched values. No raw browser storage state, cookies, passwords, databases or participant inputs belong here.

Binary review is bounded and fallible; image OCR can miss content. Supplied brand-reference slide rights and team/author metadata approvals remain unresolved. Consult [publication audit](../phase-10-publication-audit.md) before any upload, including a private repository. Do not treat private visibility as a secret-removal mechanism.
