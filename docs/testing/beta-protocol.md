# Beta protocol — shieldmate-demo-usability-1

**REAL BETA: NOT CONDUCTED. HYPOTHESIS: PROPOSED, NOT YET TEAM-ADOPTED.** No participant has been recruited or observed by this implementation. Assignment 5 requires real target users; five distinct eligible participants is the project's pragmatic proposed sample, not a course-mandated minimum. Earlier discovery interviews and developer/Playwright runs do not qualify.

## 1. Adopt and freeze before the first task

Proposed hypothesis: **At least 80% of target-user beta participants can identify the highest-priority issue and explain a sensible next action within 120 seconds, without moderator hints, using the clearly labeled fixed test scenario.** This measures usability/comprehension only. It does not establish incident reduction, comprehensive detection, ROI, willingness to pay or commercial adoption.

The team must read `scenario.json` (moderator-only answer key), approve the definition/denominator and participant wording, then record actual adoption time in its private study declaration **before trials**. Do not backdate. An unadopted study yields exploratory counts, not prospective confirmation. Keep the definition hash, application build, kit commit and participant-language sheet version in each session note. CLI checks the actual fixture, Top 1/action, numeric criterion, and participant-wording hashes; recorded `scenario_sha256` hashes the complete scenario definition, including rubric/material hashes. Changing it requires a new reviewed study version/build; preserve old records and analyze under their original checkout. Do not change the answer after seeing results. Use a clean committed application build for beta; analyze different builds separately rather than pooling a changed interface into one threshold result. The frozen definition also checks the demo UI and EN/RU/KK catalogs.

## 2. Recruit genuine target users

Recommend five distinct SME owners, operations leads, IT generalists or outsourced IT professionals actually involved in SME security decisions. Screen with one non-identifying question: “Do you currently help decide or manage security/IT actions for a small or medium business?” Record a broad role and eligibility reason, not an employer name/domain. Teammates belong in alpha; friends may qualify only through genuine target responsibility, with `relationship=close` disclosed as sampling bias. Do not recruit classmates merely acting as customers. Document actual recruitment separately and locally; **do not send invitations automatically**.

Use EN/RU/KK invitations, consent, neutral opening, task sheet and six closing questions in `materials/participant-en.md`, `participant-ru.md`, `participant-kk.md`. These are AI-assisted drafts for team/native review, not certified translations. Match the language to the participant; record it. Do not improvise easier translations or clues during the first timed task. Unknown language support must be disclosed rather than counted silently.

## 3. Consent and private setup

Use a fresh guest browser profile/context with no operator authentication or autofill. Start normal compiled release at localhost8080 and open `/demo`, which is fictional/read-only, accesses no tenant data, and performs no scans. Set language and reload to the initial assets step. Confirm readiness and the synthetic banner before beginning. The reserved domain and fixed 2026-10-01 scenario date are not a fresh assessment. Reset by a fresh context/reload; never delete a legitimate company's data. No participant account, password, real domain or company secret is needed.

Before collecting anything, the team fills moderator code, consented retention date, local custodian, withdrawal route and approved appendix audience. Core notes consent is required; screenshots/audio/exact quotations each get an independent yes/no. Refusal of media does not exclude someone. Use codes B01 etc.; keep any needed contact/code mapping separately, access-controlled, never in the CSV or repository. Do not ask for contacts when arranging an already-present session. No new telemetry, external forms or analytics is installed.

## 4. Standardized tasks and timing

Read the chosen-language opening verbatim. Begin the primary trial with `/demo` on assets, no priority panel already selected. Show/read exactly the first task. Start a monotonic timer or a consistently used manual stopwatch **when the prompt is first shown or reading begins**; stop when the participant submits the issue/action answer. Record observed seconds with decimal point and method; never infer or invent a time. A second attempt is exploratory and cannot replace the first primary trial. One primary record per distinct participant; CLI rejects duplicate IDs even with different session IDs.

**Primary success requires all:** completed first trial; correct certificate-renewal issue according to frozen rubric; at least one sensible certificate/hosting next action; no moderator hint; no pause; observed elapsed time ≤120 seconds. Exact wording, rule IDs or every certificate-chain detail are unnecessary. Clear equivalents in any supported language count; the human moderator records first, then grades. There is no automated/LLM grading. Calendar-date confusion may be captured separately; this is a fixed scenario, not today's certificate status.

After recording the first answer, use the remaining participant tasks: navigate to evidence unaided, open example work and explain Done versus evidence-confirmed Resolved, then review the marked sample PDF and its scope/dates. Public example work is read-only; do not ask participants to click a nonexistent task-edit control. Real task mutations are covered separately by human alpha/engineering in an isolated workspace. This is a narrower beta usability study, not proof that every private feature has been tested by customers.

Neutral prompts: “Please continue as you would normally”; “You may say you are unsure”; repeat the original task verbatim. Prohibited hints before answer submission: naming TLS/certificates, selecting the priorities tab, pointing to a particular issue, reading out its action, suggesting a button or praising/correcting a partial answer. If a hint occurs, record `hint_given=yes`, keep the first result and count primary failure. Help after the recorded first answer may support exploratory discussion; mark subsequent assistance accurately.

## 5. Denominator, interruptions and accessible participation

Primary N includes **all consented eligible primary starts that are not withdrawn**, including incomplete/aborted/technical-failure trials. They are failures unless all success conditions are observed. Missing/unknown time is not zero and cannot pass. A pause ends a comparable timed trial; record it and its reason privately, without subtracting pause time or silently restarting. `not_started` sessions, declined consent and ineligible screenings are reported separately. Never select only favorable completions.

Withdrawn responses/media are excluded explicitly and deleted by the team as agreed, not by the CLI. If permitted, retain only minimal coded disposition; otherwise remove the individual row and increment the declaration's **anonymized `withdrawn_started_removed`** count once. Never retain answers, timing or media references in a withdrawn row. Report withdrawn-start counts and the CLI's conservative sensitivity ratio **successes / (primary N + withdrawn starts)**. Do not double-count removed and retained withdrawal records. Withdrawal is not a participant performance failure; the sensitivity bound exposes missing-outcome uncertainty. The team must disclose any further data loss.

For five analyzed participants, 4/5 reaches80%; 3/5 does not. At least five is the proposed plan; a smaller sample cannot confirm this plan even if 100% succeeds. Report exact n/N, counts of hinted/paused/missing-time/aborted/withdrawn/not-started trials, language mix and convenience-sample limitations. Ratings are optional 1–5 distributions; missing ratings stay missing. No NPS, payment conversion, security score, breach reduction or ROI is calculated.

Offer keyboard use, text enlargement, screen readers or identical printed/read-aloud instructions before the timer; record `protocol_variant=accessible` without collecting medical/disability details. Keep the same criterion for the declared comparison and explain accessibility/time-pressure limitations. Do not exclude someone or relax the score secretly. If an accommodation changes timing/comparability, retain the original disposition and report the exploratory accommodated attempt separately, with team approval and clear labeling.

## 6. Closing, evidence and failure recovery

Ask the six chosen-language questions without leading responses. Clarity/usefulness ratings are perceptions, not purchasing behavior. Do not pressure for a quote. Record actions/error counts only when observed; describe comment provenance as verbatim-with-permission or moderator paraphrase. Review claims and grading with another team member when possible; disagreement stays documented.

If the machine/demo fails before the task, record not_started and recover before a new appointment. If it fails after timing starts, stop, preserve the observed incomplete/aborted first trial and error evidence (if consented); repair/restart and offer an exploratory follow-up, not a replacement success. If a participant is about to submit personal data, enroll a real domain or enter another workspace, stop for privacy/scope and record the interruption; do not direct them to the answer. Security/isolation failures stop the study until repaired and regressed. Never weaken controls, seed real customer data or remove volumes to recover.

Copy only genuine records to private CSVs; manifest actual dated notes/files with hashes. Validate/analyze using `README.md`. Review/redact all chosen submission proof manually; hashing proves file consistency, not human authenticity or consent. No human data, recordings, raw answers or live company domains belong in Git. Recruitment, consent, observations, timing, grading, native review and final academic conclusions remain team activities, **PENDING MANUAL EVIDENCE**.
